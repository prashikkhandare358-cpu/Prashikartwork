Philosophy & Practice

Artist Statement

"My sculptural practice emerges from lived experience, social reality, and the search for human dignity. Using found objects, natural materials, and everyday elements, I explore themes of labour, identity, equality, and memory. I see art as a space for dialogue, reflection, and transformation rather than mere representation. Through my work, I seek to create meaningful encounters that encourage viewers to question inherited structures and reconnect with human values."
